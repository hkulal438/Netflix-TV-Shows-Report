**Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard**

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Netflix’s Movies and TV Shows dataset, offering clear insights into the platform’s content distribution across ratings, genres, countries, and release years. It highlights important metrics such as total titles, total ratings, number of genres, number of directors, and the earliest and latest release years available in the dataset. Through visually rich charts, users can easily identify trends in Netflix content, such as growth in titles over time, popular genres, top content ratings, and the distribution between movies and TV shows. The dashboard enables digital media analysts, researchers, and content strategists to understand Netflix’s global content landscape and make informed decisions using real-time data visuals.

**Purpose of the Dashboard**

This dashboard helps users:

Analyze Netflix’s content library growth over time

Identify top-performing genres and categories

Understand audience ratings and age classifications

Compare Movies vs TV Shows inventory

Explore geographic distribution of Netflix’s content

Support data-driven decisions for media analysis, content planning, and entertainment research
